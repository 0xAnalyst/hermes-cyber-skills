# Red Teaming

Adversary emulation and full-scope engagement work: scoping and authorization
design, objective-based campaigns, infrastructure and opsec methodology, and
reporting that measures detection coverage rather than just access.

**No skills published here yet** — the first one lands when it is built.

## Ground rules

- **Engagement is scoped and authorized in writing before any action.** Rules of
  engagement, target list, and stop conditions precede tooling.
- **Deconfliction matters:** blue-team awareness channels and out-of-scope assets
  are defined up front, not improvised mid-campaign.
- **Third-party red-team skills are linked, not mirrored** (see the repo
  [Credits](../../README.md#credits--provenance)).

## Adding a skill here

Engagement planning and scoping, adversary-emulation playbooks mapped to
ATT&CK, C2 and redirector methodology, opsec, detection-evasion measurement.
Exploit development aimed at assessment belongs in
[`../penetration-testing`](../penetration-testing/); detection of the resulting
activity belongs in [`../dfir`](../dfir/).
